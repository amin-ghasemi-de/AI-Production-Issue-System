DOCUMENT_TYPE: action
TITLE: Recommended Actions for Injection Molding RAG
PURPOSE: Provide only actions explicitly supported by documented safety or troubleshooting guidance.
IMPORTANT: A possible cause is not automatically an action. Do not recommend an action unless a retrieved rule supports it. Machine manuals, approved factory procedures, resin supplier processing data, and site safety rules override this PoC document.

ACTION A-01 — Safety: guard or interlock problem
Condition: Missing, bypassed, defeated, or inoperable guard/interlock, or a worker can reach a danger zone while the machine can move.
Recommended action: Do not bypass the safeguard. Stop unsafe operation and restore the required guarding/interlock before normal operation.
Evidence basis: OSHA injection-molding machine-guarding guidance.

ACTION A-02 — Safety: servicing, mold access, cleaning, unjamming, or maintenance with hazardous-energy exposure
Condition: The task requires guard removal/bypass, body entry into a point of operation or danger zone, or exposure to unexpected startup or hazardous-energy release.
Recommended action: Follow the site's lockout/tagout energy-control procedure before servicing or maintenance and verify isolation before work begins.
Evidence basis: OSHA Control of Hazardous Energy guidance and injection-molding eTool.

ACTION A-03 — Safety: hot plastic or hot-surface exposure
Condition: Credible risk of burns from a heated barrel, hot surfaces, molten-plastic splatter, hot gases, or vapors.
Recommended action: Use required guarding and appropriate heat-resistant PPE and eye/face protection according to the site procedure and machine/material guidance.
Evidence basis: OSHA injection-molding safety guidance.

ACTION A-04 — Safety: damaged high-pressure hose or fitting
Condition: Hose or fitting is punctured, frayed, damaged, leaking, or otherwise capable of spraying hot oil or another substance at high pressure.
Recommended action: Remove the machine from unsafe service as required by site procedure, inspect the affected hose/fitting, and replace damaged components according to manufacturer guidance before return to normal operation.
Evidence basis: OSHA injection-molding safety guidance.

ACTION A-05 — Short shot or incomplete fill: insufficient injection pressure
Condition: Short shot/incomplete fill is present and insufficient injection pressure is supported as the cause.
Recommended action: Increase injection pressure within the machine, mold, and material supplier's approved process window.
Evidence basis: Avient troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-06 — Short shot or incomplete fill: insufficient shot size
Condition: Short shot/incomplete fill is present and shot size is insufficient.
Recommended action: Increase shot size within approved machine/process limits.
Evidence basis: Avient and Solvay troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-07 — Short shot or incomplete fill: poor venting
Condition: Short shot is associated with blocked or insufficient venting.
Recommended action: Clean, improve, or appropriately deepen/add vents according to tool-design and maintenance requirements.
Evidence basis: Avient and Solvay troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-08 — Short shot or incomplete fill: melt too cold or injection too slow
Condition: The documented process window confirms melt temperature is too low, or troubleshooting evidence indicates injection speed is too low.
Recommended action: Adjust melt temperature or injection speed toward the approved material/process window.
Safety limit: Do not raise temperature beyond the resin supplier's recommended process range.

ACTION A-09 — Flash: excessive injection pressure or speed
Condition: Flash is present and excessive injection pressure or injection speed is supported.
Recommended action: Reduce injection pressure/time or injection speed as appropriate within the approved process window.
Evidence basis: Avient and RTP troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-10 — Flash: clamp or mold issue
Condition: Flash is associated with inadequate clamp pressure/capacity, poor mold fit, dirt at the parting line, or mold damage/misalignment.
Recommended action: Check clamp pressure/capacity and inspect the mold parting line, fit, alignment, and condition. Correct the identified mold/clamp issue before continuing production.
Evidence basis: Avient and Solvay troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-11 — Burn marks: venting-related
Condition: Burn marks are associated with clogged vents, poor venting, trapped gas, or dieseling.
Recommended action: Clean and improve the affected venting system according to the mold maintenance procedure.
Evidence basis: Avient and Solvay troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-12 — Burn marks: fill rate too fast
Condition: The applicable material/process guide supports excessive fill rate as the cause.
Recommended action: Reduce injection/fill speed within the approved process window.
Evidence basis: Solvay troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-13 — Voids or sinks: inadequate packing or material
Condition: Voids/sinks are associated with inadequate material supply, pack/hold pressure, or pack/hold time.
Recommended action: Verify cushion and shot size, then adjust pack/hold pressure or time within approved process limits. Consider gate/runner adequacy when documentation supports it.
Evidence basis: Avient and Solvay troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-14 — Moisture-related defect
Condition: Splay, streaking, brittle behavior, voids, or another documented defect is associated with wet material.
Recommended action: Verify the resin drying procedure and dry the material according to the resin supplier's specified drying conditions.
Safety/quality limit: Do not invent drying temperature or time.

ACTION A-15 — Material degradation or abnormal odor/color
Condition: Off-color part, abnormal odor, or degradation is supported.
Recommended action: Check for contamination, reduce process temperature toward the approved range if excessive, reduce residence time, review regrind level, and purge the machine when supported by the material guide.
Evidence basis: Avient troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-16 — Progressively shorter shots
Condition: Shots become progressively shorter and the documented cause is excessive residence time or excessive barrel temperature.
Recommended action: Purge the machine, reduce excessive residence time/cycle exposure, and reduce barrel temperature toward the approved material range.
Evidence basis: Solvay Torlon PAI troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-17 — Part sticking or part too soft at ejection
Condition: Part sticks during ejection or ejector marks indicate the part is too soft.
Recommended action: Increase mold-closed/cooling time or reduce mold/process temperature when supported by the approved material/process window. Also inspect ejection force, ejector design, and draft if the problem persists.
Evidence basis: Avient troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-18 — Cycle too short / insufficient cooling
Condition: Documentation or the report establishes that the cycle is too short and the part is insufficiently cooled.
Recommended action: Increase mold-closed/cooling time until the part is sufficiently rigid for acceptable ejection, using material- and part-specific process requirements.
Evidence basis: Solvay and Avient processing guidance.

ACTION A-19 — Unexpectedly long cycle time due to cooling inefficiency
Condition: Cycle time is longer than the validated baseline and cooling is suspected.
Recommended action: Check cooling-system performance and inspect for inadequate coolant flow, cooling-design limitations, corrosion, or scale buildup in cooling channels. Restore cooling performance according to mold-maintenance procedures.
Evidence basis: BASF technical information on injection-molding cooling times.

ACTION A-20 — Non-uniform color
Condition: Non-uniform color is associated with poor mixing/dispersion.
Recommended action: Check barrel temperatures, back pressure, and screw rpm against the approved process setup and inspect material/regrind for contamination.
Evidence basis: Avient troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-21 — Dimensional out-of-specification parts
Condition: Dimensions are out of specification and shrinkage control is suspected.
Recommended action: Check hold pressure, melt temperature, mold temperature, and cushion against the validated setup/process window before making controlled adjustments.
Evidence basis: Avient quality-control troubleshooting guidance.

ACTION A-22 — No documented action
Condition: Retrieved documentation identifies a possible cause but does not explicitly specify a corrective action for the reported condition.
Recommended action: Return null. State that the available production documentation does not contain enough information to provide a recommended action.
Do not transform a possible cause into an invented maintenance instruction.

ACTION A-23 — Raw numeric values without documented limits
Condition: The report provides temperature, pressure, cycle time, or downtime numbers but the knowledge base does not define the correct parameter range/limit for the applicable machine, material, mold, or process.
Recommended action: Do not recommend a process adjustment from the number alone. Return that the value cannot be validated against the available documentation and request the applicable process window or machine/material limit.

ACTION A-24 — Conflicting operator comment and recorded data
Condition: An operator comment directly contradicts a documented limit or a recorded parameter that has a validated reference range.
Recommended action: Do not produce a process adjustment until the discrepancy is resolved. Flag the conflicting field and request verification of the measurement/comment.
Constraint: Do not call a comment wrong unless the contradiction is supported by documented data.

ACTION A-25 — Use a systematic troubleshooting approach
Condition: Multiple process adjustments are plausible and there is no safety emergency.
Recommended action: Compare the report against the validated setup/process window and use controlled troubleshooting changes rather than changing many variables at once.
Evidence basis: Avient recommends a systematic troubleshooting approach and restoring a previously successful process to its proper balance.

ACTION A-26 — Material-specific limits override generic troubleshooting
Condition: A resin supplier data sheet or approved material process window is available.
Recommended action: Keep temperature, pressure, drying, and timing adjustments inside the applicable supplier/factory limits.
